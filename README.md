Relevance to Engineering
This project demonstrates application of thermodynamic cycle analysis — covered in undergraduate ME curricula — to real-world automotive and aerospace propulsion systems. Key engineering insights the simulator illustrates:

Why Diesel engines achieve higher efficiency than Otto engines at equivalent displacement
The trade-off between compression ratio and knock resistance in gasoline engines
How turbine inlet temperature limits Brayton cycle performance, driving research into thermal barrier coatings and single-crystal turbine blades
The gap between ideal cycle efficiency and real engine efficiency, and the physical reasons behind it (friction, heat transfer, non-ideal combustion)
